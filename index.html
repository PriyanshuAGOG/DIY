    Questions, answered

    A few things
    before you build.

    Do I need to be good at craft?+

    Nope. If you could fold a paper plane, you can do this. Start with a 3–4 sheet car like the Supra or the M4 GT3, then graduate to the six-sheet monsters. You will get hooked.

    What do I need at home?+

    A craft knife or sharp scissors, a ruler, something to score folds with (an empty ballpoint works), and a quick-grip craft glue. Patience is optional but recommended.

    How big are the finished cars?+

    Most are 1:18 scale — around 21 to 29 cm long. The Supra MK5 and BMW M4 GT3 are 1:24. Every kit page lists the exact built size.

    How should I care for paper art?+

    Keep it out of direct sun and humidity, and dust it with a soft brush. Treat it like a houseplant that never needs watering.

How to win

Three rules.
Zero fuel.

Every car is equally fast on paper. The race is decided by your timing, not your wallet.

01

Launch on green

Hit SHIFT the moment the lights go green. React in under 0.25s for a launch boost. Jump early and you’ll eat a penalty.

02

Shift in the green

Four shifts. Each one in the green zone of the rev needle is a PERFECT shift with a tiny boost. Too early bogs you down.

03

Respect the limiter

Leave it too long and you bounce off the red line and lose speed. Fifth gear takes you home.

Online races connect your two browsers directly (peer-to-peer). A small public matchmaking server only introduces you — nothing else is stored.
